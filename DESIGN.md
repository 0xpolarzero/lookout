# Lookout: design direction ("Quiet by default")

Lookout is a glanceable edge utility. It is looked at for under a second, many times a day, and it answers one question: does anything need me? This document is the single source of truth for the design, and it describes the product as built. Where it conflicts with the code or README, this wins; the README says the same in the user's words.

Spine: **Quiet by default** (radical editing, progressive disclosure, calm empty states). Grafted from **Gauge**: one shared footer, a working mark (first an arc, now a ring: 10.1), one focus-ring recipe, drawn switch, per-surface contrast tests, Settings panes. Grafted from **Quiet Native**: concentric radii, Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast fallbacks, Differentiate Without Colour, VoiceOver containers/announcements/rotor. Quiet Native's material and light mode are deliberately deferred (section 3.1).

---

## 1. Principles

Each is testable by reading a screenshot or running a check.

1. **Glance, then act.** The bar at rest shows state, never detail: no breakdowns, names or summaries. Test: no text longer than a count appears on the resting bar.
2. **Success is silent, failure is loud.** Passing CI, read items, idle sessions and a healthy sync render with no hue, no number and no chrome. Test: with everything healthy the bar contains no colour other than neutral greys.
3. **Three hues, one meaning each.** Amber = needs you. Red = broken. Blue (accent) = unread/interactive. Green appears in exactly one place (update ready). Nothing else carries a hue: no clay, purple, project colour in the bar, or per-kind colour. Test: grep for `Theme.green|purple|claude` outside the allowed places in 2.1.
4. **State is a shape first, a colour second, never an opacity.** Every state has a distinct silhouette (filled tile, dot, ring, glyph, numeral) and a VoiceOver value. Test: the greyscale render of each bar state is distinguishable; no `.opacity(` on content to signal state.
5. **Never fade content.** A busy, read, pending or searched-over element keeps its full text token. Only a non-text status layer (the working ring) may pulse. Test: ContrastTests pass for every token pair; no dimming of live controls.
6. **Nothing the user aimed at moves.** Bar cells keep their screen position at rest, on hover, in a peek and when kept open (on the sides the first cell; on top and bottom the leading edge of the strip). Test: pixel diff of rest vs open shots (section 9).
7. **One fact, one place, one word.** A fact is stated once (glyph or number or sentence). Vocabulary is fixed (table in 4.0). Test: grep for the retired words in 4.0.
8. **Every hover affordance has a keyboard and VoiceOver path.** Test: every row action and bar cell has an `accessibilityAction`/key; no control exists only on hover.
9. **Empty and error states are content with a cause.** "All caught up" appears only when true. Test: each row of the table in 4.9 has a playground shot.
10. **Idle is 0%.** The only repeating motion is the working ring, drawn by Core Animation. Test: `scripts/idle-cpu.sh` (section 8).

## 3. Tokens

All live in `Theme`/`Theme.*` (Theme.swift, Components.swift). A token nothing uses is deleted (there is no `Theme.raised`, `Theme.hover`, card fill, eyebrow/caption/badge font or `Radius.panel`).

### 3.1 Appearance decision

Dark-only, opaque, on purpose. Every status hue and contrast ratio is tuned for dark; light mode doubles the QA surface. All `FloatingPanel`s and their hosting views set `appearance = NSAppearance(named: .darkAqua)` so AppKit children (context menus, pop-ups, field editor, caret, selection, focus ring) match; remove nothing else. The surface stays opaque `Theme.bg`: text never sits on a wallpaper, the shape-only shadow and the bar/peek join depend on it. **Reduce Transparency is therefore a documented no-op.** A material (`NSVisualEffectView` `.popover`, `.behindWindow`, `.active`, resizable `maskImage` clip, `bg`@.72 tint, mask-out shadow, opaque fallback on Reduce Transparency/Increase Contrast) is a gated later experiment, not part of this work. Lookout follows no private text-size setting (macOS has none); rows are sized by line boxes. The accent is a fixed blue rather than `controlAccentColor` on purpose: a user accent of orange or red would collide with amber = needs you and red = broken.

**ContrastTests** (Tests/LookoutTests): composites every foreground token, as the views use it, over every surface token it can land on (bg, rail, hover, field, tile, selected, pressed, popover; amber and green fills for `onTint`) in normal and Increase Contrast sets. Text pairs ≥ 4.5:1, glyph/ring/border pairs ≥ 3:1. The pair list lives in the test; keep it in step with the tokens.

### 3.3 Type

SF Pro (system). SF Rounded only for tile letters. Floor 11 pt; no half-point sizes; `.monospacedDigit()` for counts, ages and elapsed.

| Name | Spec | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `title` | 13 semibold | page, section and group titles; unread row titles |
| `body` | 13 regular | read row titles, form labels, menu labels |
| `control` | 12 medium | tabs, buttons, chips |
| `meta` | 11 regular | second lines, summaries, hints, tooltip detail |
| `label` | 11 semibold, sentence case, `secondary` | group and form section headings (replaces tracked caps) |
| `numeral` | 11 semibold, monospaced digits | counts, ages, elapsed, status words |
| `tile` | 11 bold, rounded | tile letters: two characters at most (upper-cased before they are cut; generated ones past the digits take another letter), and the font never scales down |
| `keyhint` | 11 regular, SF Pro, `secondary` | key equivalents as plain text (never SF Rounded: ⌃ must render as ⌃) |

Unread = semibold + dot + VoiceOver value ("Unread"). Read = regular at `text`. Meta line uses `tertiary`; summaries, ages, status words and hints use `secondary`; `tertiary` is never used for a hint on a form.

### 3.4 Space, size, radius

- Space: `hair` 2, `xs` 4, `sm` 6, `md` 8, `lg` 12, `xl` 16.
- Pitch: **36 pt on both axes** for every bar cell (tile 26 centred), the header, one-line rows and the footer. Bar depth 46. Divider slot 19 pt along the axis (9 + 1 + 9), hairline 18 pt long.
- Rows: two-line **44** (inbox, CI, session); session with a task line **60**; menu rows **28** (menus and the suggestions' popover only: a row that stays on screen, Passing's repository links included, is one pitch); form rows min **36**, a Custom checkbox's too.
- Content edge: leading text x = inset 6 + row padding 8 = **14 pt**, used by headers, tabs, dots, group labels, form titles (one `Metrics.contentEdge`). Dot slot 14, avatar 24, trailing age column 36.
- Radii, all `.continuous`, concentric (inner = outer − inset): **hub 16** (bar, peek, expanded: one value, no 20/22 pop); **row 10** (= 16 − 6; also banner, undo line, settings group); **field 8**; **tile and button 7** (26 × .27); **keycap/small chip 4**; capsules for tabs. Delete `Radius.panel`/`lg + 2`. Use `ConcentricRectangle` under `#available(macOS 26, *)` only if it reproduces these numbers; otherwise the formula.
- Widths (`HubGeometry`): peek inbox 400, CI 360, sessions 400, controls 240; kept-open side hub detail 420; horizontal kept-open: left column 420 (inbox + CI), right column 400, gutter 12, max total 860; a focused section is one column: inbox ≤ 560, CI 420, sessions 480; a page is 420 beside the bar and 480 under the strip. Max hub length from `visibleFrame` minus both insets and the Dock/menu bar, never a floor.
- Hit targets: ≥ 24×24 for any control via `.contentShape` without changing the visual; icon buttons and row action buttons 24 visual / 28 hit (the style's own hit frame, so a click in the 28 lands); tag remove and shortcut reset 24.
- Surface: `bg` fill, 1 pt `stroke` outline, drawn once as one clip + one stroke. Shadows are static shapes in a layer under everything: contact (r2, y1, black .35) always; ambient (r20, y7, black .30) fixed radius, cross-faded by opacity (0 at rest, 1 on peek/open). Radius and offset never animate.

### 3.5 Focus and selection (defined once, `Components.swift`)

- **Pointer hover**: `Fill.hover` only. Cleared on pointer exit unless the last input was keyboard. A key never acts on a row the pointer left.
- **Keyboard pick** (rows): `Fill.selected` + 2 pt × 24 pt `accent` leading bar at x = 2. The bar is 6.35:1 on `bg`. In a list that floats on the popover (the repository suggestions) the fills sit on a lighter surface, so the bar is `accentText` (3:1 there under Increase Contrast too) and the owner's prefix `secondary`; ContrastTests composites the picked and pressed fills over the popover.
- **`.focusRing(radius)`**: 1.5 pt `accent` stroke, offset 2 (tiles, icon buttons, tabs, chips, menu rows, fields, switches), drawn from `@FocusState`/Full Keyboard Access focus so Tab shows position. Replaces the white .85 tile ring. Inset (offset 0, radius 10) for rows.
- Text fields: system focus behaviour plus the 1.5 pt `accent` border.

### 3.6 Motion

Three speeds, no bounce anywhere. All through `.motion(_:value:)` / `Theme.Motion.resolve(reduce)` (one entry point). `reduceNow` (NSWorkspace) remains the only non-view path, behind one `Hub.animate(_:body)` helper.

| Name | Curve | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `hover` | easeOut .12 | fills, tints |
| `fade` | easeOut .14 | content in/out, list changes by revision, undo line, sibling page switch, panel first open/last close (8 pt slide) |
| `move` | spring .28, bounce 0 | expand, page slide-in from the gear, focus |
| `close` | spring .20, bounce 0 | collapse |
| `heartbeat` | easeInOut 1.2 s autoreverse, opacity 1.0 ↔ 0.65 | the working ring only |

Rules: animate only what changed spatially or in state; no motion on hover except fills; no motion on poll refresh except row cross-fades. **Reduce Motion**: nothing translates, scales or springs, which includes scrolling to a row, a list's rows coming, going and moving, and a list changing height (instant, `listMotion`; the shortened fade is for opacity only); changes are 0.10 s cross-fades or instant; the ring is static at opacity 1; update download ring is determinate (allowed); the edge-drag glide is skipped (frame set directly). There is no `.snappy`, badge pop, linear tween, fixed transition or push between Settings panes.

`Theme.Timing` (documented, one place): dwell 100 ms (first panel only), section switch instant, leave grace 120 ms, tooltip 400 ms then instant when another tip closed < 600 ms ago.

## 4. Components

Shared ones live in Components.swift; each has rest / hover / pressed / picked (focus) / disabled states unless noted. Disabled = no hover fill, label at `tertiary` and nothing faded: no opacity multiplier on a button, so a label never falls under its pair in ContrastTests. A control that says what it is doing (the footer's "Checking…") is not disabled for it: a press while it works is ignored.

### 4.0 Vocabulary (grep-enforced)

Inbox tabs: **Needs you / Bots / Done**. Sessions: **Waiting / Working / Finished** (never "done"), groups **Waiting for you / <project> / Scratch / New activity**. CI: **Failing / Running / Passing / No runs** from one table on `CIState` (title, label, symbol, VoiceOver phrase); a repository GitHub has not answered for is **Not checked**, never "No runs" (that is for a successful answer that found none). Actions: **Keep open** (never Pinned/Unpin), **Hide** (never Remove), **Keep** (a session), **Done / Restore**. Retired words in UI strings: pending, Remove (session), Pinned, Unpin, "No checks", "No CI shown" (replaced by "No CI configured"), "Make room for this".

### 4.1 `BarCell` and `StatusTile`

`BarCell`: 36 pt slot, 26 pt tile or glyph, centred; the only element type on the bar. Accessibility: button trait, label, value, hint, action **Show** (keeps the hub open and moves selection and VoiceOver focus to that section). `StatusTile`: 26 pt, radius 7, `Fill.tile`, label `tile` font in `text`, or SF Symbol 12 pt, or emoji.

| State | Tile | Mark | Non-colour cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waiting | solid `amber`, `onTint` label | none | whole-tile luminance jump (+ inner stroke under Differentiate) |
| Working (also finished with tasks running) | `Fill.tile`, `text` | **ring**: a full rounded outline 1.5 pt `secondary` (`text` under Increase Contrast), 2 pt outside the tile, concentric (radius 7 + 3.5), breathing on the heartbeat | ring shape + motion (static opacity 1 under Reduce Motion) |
| Finished, unread | `Fill.tile`, `text` | 7 pt `accent` dot on the tile's top-trailing corner (offset +5/−5: the ring's corner when it has one) with 1.5 pt `bg`/`rail` halo | dot shape |
| Idle / read | `Fill.tile`, `text` | none | |

Waiting never shows a ring or a dot; working + unread shows both. A finished turn that ended on a question is waiting until you read it; read, it is not amber any more and the ring returns for whatever it left running. Picked/hovered: `.focusRing(7)`. New activity tiles are identical (no shrink, no dim). The ring is a cached tiny image on a `CALayer` driven by `Pulse`; every instance uses one shared `beginTime` (`layer.convertTime(CACurrentMediaTime(), from: nil)` modulo the period) so all rings breathe in phase and a re-render never restarts it; none runs while the window is hidden. The Pulse cache key includes contrast + differentiate state. Waiting/Working/Unread values: `"waiting for you, lcu, 4 minutes"`.

### 4.2 `SectionHeader` (36 pt)

`label`/`title` text, optional single status phrase (only when the section is folded to its header: "1 waiting", "1 failing"; open, its own rows say it), trailing actions. The **whole header is the focus toggle** (pointer cursor, `accessibilityAction("Focus")`, ⌘1/2/3). A `chevron.down`/`chevron.up` (24 pt target, `tertiary`, tooltip "Expand Inbox") appears only on header hover or keyboard focus; the header is a button behind its content (the Inbox's tabs included), so Tab reaches it, with the ring and the tip, on every header. A focused section shows `esc` as `keyhint` text beside the title. Trailing slots are reserved per section so tabs and right edge never jump. Title is `.isHeader`.

### 4.3 `Tabs` (inbox filters, Settings panes)

Custom drawn (a non-key panel draws `Picker(.segmented)` grey). Height 24, capsule, `control` 12. Selected = `text` on `Fill.tile` with a 1 pt `fieldBorder` outline (on a circular capsule: the continuous one's stroke grows a flat stub at each end) (the fill alone is 1.3:1 on the page; the outline holds the state at 3:1, WCAG 1.4.11), `.isSelected`; unselected = `secondary`. Container `accessibilityElement(children: .contain)` labelled "Inbox filter". Counts are `numeral` text after the label: Needs you unread in `amber`, Bots unread in `tertiary`, Done none. Badge colour encodes urgency, never selection. On a Settings page ←/→ switch panes.

### 4.4 `Row` (inbox, CI, session)

Fixed 44 pt (session task line adds 16), whole-row `Button`, hover/pick per 3.5, context menu, `.accessibilityElement(children: .combine)`.
`[14 dot slot][24 avatar or glyph] title (line 1) … age (line 1 trailing, 36 pt column) / meta (line 2) … action (line 2 trailing)`.
**Actions sit at the trailing end of line 2**, so title, age and status never disappear. One visible action per row (24 visual / 28 hit circle, `Fill.selected`), shown on hover, keyboard pick **and** VoiceOver focus. Everything else lives in the context menu, a key and `accessibilityAction`s. Tooltip (`.help`): title + first 3 lines of snippet, also for the keyboard-picked row. Every secondary line joins its parts with ` · ` (`lcu · Should updates install…`, `Running swift test · +2 more`).

### 4.5 `IconButton`

24 pt visual, 28 hit, 14 pt glyph `secondary`, hover `Fill.hover` circle, `.focusRing`. Mandatory accessibility label. Outline symbols at rest; `.fill` only for on/active (pin, bookmark).

### 4.6 `Tip`

Kept only for icon-only bar and header controls (and, for the row the keyboard picked, its title and first lines, which `.help` can't show for a key); anything with visible text uses `.help`. A tip for a cell of a bar on a side edge goes beside the cell, level with its centre and off the rail, so it never covers the neighbouring cells; along the top and bottom it is over or under the cell. Style after the system tip: popover fill #2B2B2D, radius 7, 1 pt `stroke`, padding 8/4, 12 pt label + 12 pt `secondary` key on one line ("Settings  ⌘,"), 400 ms then instant, also on keyboard focus after 1 s (every icon control passes its own focus to its tip: the cells of the bar, New session, the Inbox's More menu, the header buttons). Hoverable (WCAG 1.4.13): the bubble takes the pointer, and the tip closes only a leave grace after the pointer is on neither the control nor the bubble, so crossing the gap to it, or panning a magnifier onto it, keeps it; the window takes the mouse over the bubble and the way to it (`HubGeometry.takesMouse`), though both lie outside the hub's own frame. Dismissable: Esc closes an open tip before anything else (before the search field's own Esc too, so a picked result's tip goes before the query does), leaving the pointer, the pick and the focus, and it shows again only when a control is entered anew. A long title wraps inside the window (at most a sentence's width, never past it) and the bubble is clamped to the window on both axes (over its control when it fits there, else under it, else as low as the window lets it); its detail stops at twelve lines, fewer in a short window, and what is cut is in the control's `.help` and its VoiceOver hint. A row the keyboard picked before it mounted shows its tip too. Never carries primary information.

### 4.7 `MenuRow` and `KeyCap`

`MenuRow` matches NSMenu: 28 pt, 16 pt symbol column, 13 pt label, key equivalent as plain `keyhint` right-aligned, highlight `Fill.selected` + `.focusRing`, arrow-key navigation, Return activates, Esc closes. `KeyCap` survives only for the `esc` hint and footer key hints: SF Pro 11, no stroke, `Fill.tile`, radius 4.

### 4.8 `SwitchStyle`, `FieldStyle`, `SegmentedTabs`

`SwitchStyle`: drawn 32×20 capsule, on = `accent` + white 16 pt knob with a 1 pt `onTint` edge (white on the accent is 2.9:1, 2.5:1 hovered; the edge holds the knob's side at 3:1), off = `switchOff` (its outline too on a circular capsule); independent of key-window state; real `Toggle` for VoiceOver (value On/Off); whole label row is the hit target (≥ 36 pt); `hover` fade, instant under Reduce Motion. `FieldStyle`: 28 pt, radius 8, `Fill.field`, `fieldBorder`; focus per 3.5; secrets are `SecureField`. Buttons: bordered = `Fill.tile` + `fieldBorder`, radius 7, 28 pt, `control` font.

### 4.9 `StatusBanner`, `EmptyBlock`, `UndoLine`

`StatusBanner`: 30 pt, radius 10, `Fill.group`, icon + sentence + up to two text/bordered buttons; used for sync, rate-limit, snooze, Claude notices. `EmptyBlock`: centred two lines, min height 132 (the Sessions list's "No Claude sessions" is a quiet line instead, 5.9); line 1 `body` medium `secondary`, line 2 `meta` `tertiary`, optional one bordered button or `accentText` link; no glyph unless it carries a cause. `UndoLine`: 32 pt strip at the bottom of a section (inside the room the layout gave its list, which leaves it out: the sessions' too, in a peek as in a kept-open or focused list), `Fill.group`, radius 10, "Moved to Done · Undo", visible 6 s, ⌘Z works while visible and 30 s after, `fade`, posts a VoiceOver announcement; one-shot timer cancelled on dismiss. Used by Done, Done all, Clear Done, Hide, Mute folder, Mute CI, Stop showing CI, a repository's preset and Stop watching (the last two on the Repositories page).

### 4.10 `SearchField`

### 5.9 Empty, loading, error, snoozed, update states

One at a time, in priority order. Lists never show spinners or skeletons. Sync health is one calculation (`Store.syncFault`) behind the in-list banner, the footer, the gear's badge and Settings' sync row (the footer reads its faults from it, it doesn't repeat them), and every enabled source (repositories, review requests failed or cut short, the rate limit, staleness) is in it. Staleness is the last sync's and CI's: a sync that refreshed the inbox but left CI's oldest check three polls old is "CI isn't up to date" (amber, the gear, the footer, Settings, and "Nothing new" for an empty inbox) beside CI's own "Last checked 12:03"; the gear waits on each answer's deadline and follows Check every. A repository that was stopped is no longer in it: removal clears its fault, and an answer arriving after it is ignored, its items, cursors and notifications included (the answer is kept only for a repository still watched, from the same time, following the same events). A repository's conversations and its CI record their failures apart; the health is what they come to, and the conversations' result is published as soon as it is recorded, whether CI is on or not (with CI off there is no check to do it, and the fault would never come or go). A review search that outlives the switch of Review requests adds nothing and says nothing (the switch is counted), and one cut short on its first answer still arms the notifications: its answer is the baseline, requests last touched before it are backlog turning up late, later ones are news, until a complete search lists them all. A check of CI that finishes without changing a status still moves the gear's wait on the oldest check (an observed revision). A snooze that runs out ends for everything that reads it at its deadline (one cancellable one-shot; the banner, Resume and the Notifications row go). A review search that fails on a later page keeps the requests the earlier pages found.

## 6. Interactions and keyboard

### 6.1 Hover choreography (must survive)

1. First panel opens after a 100 ms dwell. 2. Switching sections is instant. 3. Leaving has a 120 ms grace so bar-to-panel travel never closes it. 4. Hover state no body reads stays out of observed state. 5. The hit region includes the bar/panel seam. 6. Nothing in the bar moves on hover. 7. Every panel also opens from activation (Return/VoiceOver on its bar cell).

### 6.3 Focus model

Two layers: **row selection** (↑↓, the keyboard pick) and the **chrome ring** (Tab). Pointer hover is separate and never moves VoiceOver focus. Opening from the keyboard (shortcut or `Show`) moves VoiceOver focus into the hub; Esc moves it back out and restores the previous app. Clicking the Inbox cell with another section focused gives the sections their room back first, so the picked row is on screen. The root announces on open ("Lookout, 5 need you, 1 CI failing, 1 session waiting") and on `Back to bar`.

## 8. Performance rules (idle stays 0%)

**Forbidden**: any SwiftUI-driven repeating animation (`repeatForever`, `TimelineView`, `Timer.publish`, per-view timers); looping motion outside Core Animation; a second ticking clock; new global event monitors (the only one allowed is `flagsChanged` for the lone-modifier shortcut; no key or mouse monitors at rest; the hover trigger at rest is a tracking area); animating shadow radius/offset; `drawingGroup`/blur/mask around lists or live content; spinners, shimmer, skeletons; a re-render of the whole hub on poll (rows cross-fade by revision; the sync state changes colour/glyph without spinning; nor may a check of CI that moves the oldest check's time redraw it: the stale line's room is a flag).

**Required**: the only looping element is the working ring (shared `beginTime`, one cached image per appearance, removed when the window is occluded, at most 30 frames a second: a slow fade needs no more, and at the display's 120 Hz the render server would composite it four times as often; holding it static under Low Power Mode was considered and not done, as the ring is the cue that something is working). Time labels go through `Ticking`/`Clock`; add `Clock.minute` (30 s) for ages and "Checked 2m ago", due at an absolute time that a change of the timer's pace keeps (the first tick after one is that time, not a whole step away, so scrolling a working row in and out never postpones an age past its 30 s), and a label handed from the seconds clock to this older one keeps the system's time from the handoff until it catches up (an elapsed time never goes back); seconds tick only for a visible working session under a minute; the 1 s clock timer is not running when no seconds-ticking view is on screen. A label mounting redraws no other label: only the first one to ask starts the clock and sets its time, a later one draws its first frame from the system's date, and the footer keeps one clock view whatever its line says. Undo lines use one-shot timers cancelled on dismissal. Whole-row lazy lists, hover state out of the body, 0.5 s coalesced saves, FSEvents watchers, 60 s poll (30 s open, ×2 in Low Power) hold. A 304 returns the value decoded from the body it stands for (kept with the ETag, per key and type), not a new parse; the ETag cache is sized from the repository count at each poll (about eight answers each) so a poll never evicts what it is about to ask for, and a new commit's answers replace the old one's.

Instruments (SwiftUI + Core Animation, no frame over 8 ms while opening) is guidance for manual profiling, not a gate. The sync state (the footer's line, the controls peek's row, the empty inbox's cause) is read in views of their own, so a poll, which sets `isSyncing` and `lastSync` every time, redraws those and not the hub; GitHub's remaining count, which moves with every answer, is not read by the hub's body either, only whether it ran out (`Store.rateLimited`); a test tracks the hub's body through poll-shaped mutations. A selection is observed row by row: the hub keeps a light for each row (`Lights`: the pick, the keyboard's pick, the hovered session), written only for the row let go and the row lit, and a row reads its own, so a hover step redraws two rows and not every row of every list; a session row does not read the store's cache for its move actions (its group says them), and a search's matches are counted from a memo of ids that only the sessions and what is kept move. A tick redraws the age text and a screen reader's label, each from a `Ticking` of its own (`spokenTime`, which follows the clock only while VoiceOver is on), never the row; a `Ticking` reads the clock only while it is on screen and is redrawn once when it comes back; the clock changing pace does not touch what labels read, only starting after a stop does. The bar's age clock follows VoiceOver through the environment, which observes it. What changes often is read by the views that draw it: an update download's progress (whole percents, its own property, the phase and `showsInPill` change only when they do) and a working session's activity, tasks and turns (read in `Scoped` bodies beside the list's own rows), each with a test that writes it with the hub pinned or not.

**Gates** (section 9 runs them):
1. `scripts/idle-cpu.sh`: launches the release build with `--demo agents --lifecycle` (the demo's data with the real lifecycle: the demo's working sessions stay on the bar, so the rings breathe and their rows tick; polling whose every request fails at once; and the Claude watchers, reads and timer on an empty temporary folder, whose answer is not applied, as it would take the demo's sessions away) on the right edge and along the top (it puts the bar on screen for about three minutes, so it is the final gate, not a per-change one), waits 5 s to settle, checks that the app reports working sessions and rings on stdout (`lifecycle: sessions=N working=W rings=R showing=0|1 reduceMotion=0|1`, said once and again whenever it changes: no ring looping, no verdict), at the start and at any moment of the window, samples the process's cumulative CPU time at t0 and t0+30 s (`ps -o cputime=`, converted to seconds), asserts Δ/30 s < 0.1% once at rest and once kept open (`--open`), prints WindowServer's share separately, and measures it once more on `--demo busy` (no working session) to print what the rings cost it. It fails when no session works, when no ring has its loop attached or Reduce Motion is on (a locked or asleep screen, or a covered bar, removes the ring's animation, and the number would be 0 for nothing; the app says it, the window list does not: it lists covered windows), and when a sample is missing or malformed, the window is not above zero or the CPU time goes back. A second run, `--demo agents --lifecycle --canned`, answers GitHub from memory (`CannedGitHub`: a quiet account's answers sized as GitHub sizes them, each with an ETag, a 304 for any request that has it, polled every 5 s) and asserts the same limit on the rest figure plus what the canned run costs above it, scaled (the rest of the process does not poll faster), so a poll's requests, cache and bookkeeping are measured. It does **not** cover: the network itself (TLS, the radio, what a changed answer costs to parse and draw), notifications, the updater's loop, real session files changing under the watchers, transcripts being read, typing, hovering and scrolling.
2. Geometry: unit tests over the layout (`HubGeometry`) and a pixel check of rest vs open shots.
3. `ContrastTests`, `swift test`.
4. Grep gate in `scripts/design-lint.sh`: forbidden symbols (`repeatForever`, `TimelineView`, `Timer.publish`, `addGlobalMonitorForEvents` other than `.flagsChanged`, `.snappy`, `bounce: 0.0[1-9]`, `.system(size: [0-9.]*` below 11, retired words in 4.0).

## 10. Decisions after the first integration

Numbered because the code cites them. Sections 3 to 8 already say each one; this is where they are in one place.

1. **Working mark is a ring, not an arc.** A 270° arc hugging the tile read as a bracket. Working (and finished with tasks running) = a full rounded-rect outline 1.5 pt, `secondary` (Increase Contrast: `text`), drawn 2 pt outside the tile (concentric radius 7 + 3.5), breathing on the shared heartbeat (opacity 1 ↔ 0.65, 1.2 s, one shared `beginTime`; 0.65 is the lowest at which the ring still holds 3:1 on the rail, a hovered row and a picked one: ContrastTests walks both ends); static at opacity 1 under Reduce Motion. Waiting never has the ring. The unread dot sits on the ring's corner.
2. **Peeks don't repeat the bar.** In a sessions peek the rows carry no tile column: the bar's tiles are right there. Hovering or picking a row lights its bar tile with `.focusRing(7)`; hovering a tile lights its row. Kept-open (where the rail tiles move beside their rows) is unchanged.
3. **Peeks never scroll.** Every peek shows whole rows up to its cap and ends with one `+N more` row that keeps the hub open focused on that section. Kept-open lists scroll.
4. **One overflow phrase and one cap rule.** `+N more` everywhere (no "Show all N"). The bar's `+N` tile and the lists use the same rule (waiting sessions are never collapsed; the remaining visible slots are shared in panel order), so the numbers agree.
5. **Header phrases only when they add information.** "1 waiting" (Sessions) and "1 failing" (CI) appear in a section header only when that section is collapsed to its header (another section focused, or search). Expanded, the Waiting for you group and the failing rows already say it. Inbox tab counts stay (the list may be cut).
6. **Search keeps the bar intact.** The CI block leaves the kept-open layout during search but its bar cell stays in the rail at its place.
7. **Meta separators.** Every secondary line joins its parts with ` · ` (e.g. waiting rows: `lcu · Should updates install…`).
8. **One health, one overflow, one order.** Sync health is `Store.syncFault` for the banner, the footer, the gear and Settings (review requests and an incomplete search included). The Sessions list is complete when it is the focused section, and every surface that lists sessions (bar, peek, list, keys) follows the same cap and the same frozen order. Content reads in the same order on every edge: along the bottom only the strip moves to the edge. The working ring's trough is 0.65, the lowest that holds 3:1 (10.1).
9. **Review round 2.** The search lists every session that matches (the list scrolls). The bar and the sessions list cut the same sessions from one arrangement, a frozen group counts all of its own, and a cut says how many of what is below waits. The freeze is the hub's, not the bar cells'. Sync health includes a cut-short review search and no longer a repository that was stopped. Bound actions come before typing and the hub's own chords. A selected row has a keyboard route to its context menu, the bar a route that is not a drag (Settings' Bar group). The selected tab and the switch's knob hold 3:1 by an outline and an edge. Spoken ages follow the clock they are drawn from.
10. **Review round 3.** One health includes CI's staleness. A configured key beats the arrows and the row's menu chords on every row, and a focused control keeps the keys it is using. A menu key equivalent is a key SwiftUI can name. The bar's `+N` lists every session. The sessions shortcut picks the first of Waiting for you. The side hub has an inset against the rail as against the outline, and its lists take overlay scrollers, so the tiles stay on the rail's axis. A search group with nothing is a line, and the other spans the body. Tooltips are hoverable. Disabled is a step down in token, never a fade. Display placement is by identity. Progress and activity are read by the views that draw them, not the hub.
11. **Review round 4.** The bottom hub reads in the same order as the others: its footer follows the content, just above the strip. Rows that stay on screen keep the 36 pt pitch (Passing's links, the Custom checkboxes). A global shortcut another app holds keeps the old one working, and a token or a label that is refused says so. Same-named projects tell themselves apart by path. Tooltips can be dismissed, reached from outside the hub and bounded by the window; one the keyboard picked before it mounted shows. A search result or a failed update check is announced where it happens, and a snooze or an answer that is no longer current stops being true at once. Stopped repositories add nothing late; CI feeds the sync health; Keep all keeps what its header lists. Show controls and Show on CI have somewhere to go. A cut inbox with no room for its line says it in its header. A selection, a session's step and a clock tick each redraw only what they change.
12. **Review round 5.** Same-named projects are told apart wherever a project is named, from one set of names worked out when the folders change. A move acts on the row the list shows beside the row, and the held order moves with it. Focused CI opens Passing and Passing still closes. The sessions' overflow row has its `+N` tile in the rail. Reduce Motion makes a list's rows instant. Restore defaults is all or nothing. A clock's change of pace keeps the minute's deadline, and an elapsed time never goes back. A late CI failure or review search does not outlive the switch it belongs to; a review search cut short still arms notifications; the gear follows a check that changed nothing. Retry is 28 pt, capsule outlines are circular, and the idle gate reads from the app that a ring loops and refuses a sample it could not take.
13. **Review round 6.** A repository's conversations feed the sync health with CI off too. An empty search says "No match" once, and Esc closes a tooltip before it clears the query. Icon controls show their tip on keyboard focus, and a tall tip stays in the window. A token GitHub rejects is announced. A section the app doesn't offer can't be focused. Move up and down only trade places with rows the list shows. The sessions' undo line is in the list's room and in peeks. Leaving the hub ends the search field too. The update tile's Return does its label; VoiceOver's press on CI and a session keeps the hub open. Add adds what the field reads. The bar's CI glyph says "not checked" as the list does. A CI check no longer redraws the hub's body. Project names, 304s and the ETag cache cost what they should at rest, and the idle gate polls through 304s.
